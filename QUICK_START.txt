🚀 GETTING STARTED:

1. EXPLORE THE CODE:
   - Open: app/page.tsx (Homepage - 471 lines, well-commented)
   - Open: app/projects/page.tsx (Projects page - 425 lines)
   - Open: app/university-notes/page.tsx (Notes page - 373 lines)
   - Open: app/globals.css (Theme colors - 210 lines)

2. READ THE GUIDES:
   - IMPLEMENTATION_SUMMARY.md - What was built
   - PORTFOLIO_SETUP.md - How to customize

3. CUSTOMIZE YOUR PORTFOLIO:
   - Replace "Your Name" with your actual name
   - Update email and social links
   - Add your projects data
   - Add your university courses
   - Update theme colors

4. RUN LOCALLY:
   pnpm dev
   
   Visit: http://localhost:3000
